Founder & President

Adv. Sangeeta Hiralal Desarda

Founder & President, Arihant Foundation
Advocate, Bombay High Court — Aurangabad Bench

Adv. Sangeeta Hiralal Desarda is a practicing advocate and social-impact founder committed to justice, human dignity, gender equality, climate action and community empowerment.

As a first-generation lawyer, her professional journey has been shaped by a belief that law must ultimately serve people — particularly those who face barriers in accessing rights, opportunity and institutional justice.

Her work through Arihant Foundation extends this philosophy beyond the courtroom into education, women and girls' empowerment, environmental sustainability, community resilience, legal literacy and youth engagement.

The Foundation reflects a broader conviction: lasting change is created when knowledge, dignity and opportunity reach communities at the grassroots.

“Leadership is not about visibility alone, but impact.”
