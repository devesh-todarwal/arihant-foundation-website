Our Work · 02 — Children & Education

Education opens futures

We create opportunities for children to learn, participate, express themselves and develop the confidence to imagine and build a better future.

Why it matters

Education is the greatest leveler — and this generation's greatest edge. Many children need more than classroom instruction: they need spaces where they can ask questions, express themselves and learn with confidence.

What we do

  • Evening learning circles bringing girls and boys together for lessons, stories and shared learning
  • Creative activities — art, crafts and the Diwali diya workshops
  • School-linked campaigns, including the Community Seed Bank at Badak-wasti School
  • Shared meals that turn learning into belonging

Where we work

Our work is rooted in Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar (Aurangabad) and the surrounding villages of Marathwada — including Badak-wasti, where several of our initiatives have taken shape.
