International Engagement

Local Action. Global Conversations.

Arihant Foundation believes that grassroots experience has an important place in wider conversations on sustainable development, climate justice, gender equality and human rights.

The Foundation and its leadership have participated in and engaged with international forums, initiatives and civil-society networks addressing these issues.

This engagement seeks to bring community perspectives into wider discussions while learning from experiences and approaches emerging across different regions.

Engagement

Areas of engagement

Gender Equality
Participation in international conversations and initiatives addressing women's rights and economic justice.
Climate Governance
Engagement with discussions concerning climate finance, equity, resilience and the Global South.
Disaster Risk Reduction
Engagement with knowledge and leadership initiatives concerning community resilience and disaster risk reduction.
Sustainable Development
Connecting grassroots action with broader sustainable-development priorities.
