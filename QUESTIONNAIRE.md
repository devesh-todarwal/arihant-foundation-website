# 🌱 The Questionnaire — Let's Tell Your NGO's Story

Hi! This is the heart of the whole project. The website will only feel *genuine and moving*
if it's built from real answers — your mother's actual words, real stories, real numbers.

**How to answer:** Just type your answers right under each question (in this file, or paste
into the chat — whichever is easier). Don't worry about writing polished prose; rough notes
are perfect. I'll shape them into beautiful copy. Skip anything you don't know yet and we'll
circle back. Wherever helpful, I've put *example-style hints in italics/brackets*.

The **⭐ starred** questions are the ones that matter most for warmth and relatability —
please give those a little extra love.
