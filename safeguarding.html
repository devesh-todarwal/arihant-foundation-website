Child Safeguarding

Every child has the right to safety, dignity and privacy.

Arihant Foundation is committed to protecting the dignity, safety, privacy and wellbeing of every child who participates in or is represented through its programmes.

We seek to ensure that children are treated with respect and that their participation, photographs and personal information are handled responsibly.

Our approach is guided by the principle that every child has the right to safety, dignity, privacy, education and an environment in which they can learn and grow.

Read Our Child Safeguarding Policy

The full policy document will be published here once finalised and approved by the Foundation.

Questions or concerns relating to child safety in our programmes can be raised directly with the Foundation at arihantfoundation@gmail.com.
