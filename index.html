What We Believe

A better world is not a dream to wait for. It is a choice — pro-nature, pro-poor and pro-women — made real by the consistent effort of ordinary people.

Arihant Foundation brings together communities, social workers, educators, human-rights advocates and young people to address inequality, environmental challenges and barriers to opportunity — not with despair, but with practical, community-led solutions.

We believe lasting change begins when people have knowledge, dignity, opportunity and a voice in the decisions that affect their lives.

Celebrating the Badak-wasti Water Initiative
Field Story · Water

The water that reopened the schools

The Challenge

In Badak-wasti, dry conditions had made access to water a serious daily challenge for the community. Water scarcity affects far more than household needs — it can affect education, health, livelihoods and community life.

Our Response

Arihant Foundation supported a borewell water-line initiative to improve access to water for the community.

The Change

Improved water access helped create better conditions for community life and education, including the reopening of local schooling.

What Comes Next

Sustainable community management and responsible water use remain essential to ensuring that the benefits continue beyond the initial intervention.

“Water first. Everything else follows.”

Field Story · Arihant Community Seed Bank

The children who guard the seeds

The Challenge

Traditional and indigenous seed varieties carry agricultural knowledge, biodiversity and resilience. Yet this heritage can be lost when local knowledge and seed diversity are not passed from one generation to the next.

Our Response

Through the Arihant Community Seed Bank, children and community members are encouraged to learn about indigenous seeds, their value, preservation and their connection to sustainable agriculture.

The Change

The initiative connects education with climate resilience, biodiversity and community heritage — helping young people become custodians of knowledge that belongs to generations before them.

What Comes Next

We aim to strengthen community participation, expand awareness around indigenous and climate-resilient seeds, and connect education with sustainable agricultural practices.

“Every seed we preserve carries a piece of our future.”

Our Story

It began at home.

Arihant Foundation began with a simple belief: meaningful social change does not always begin with large institutions. It can begin with people who decide to listen, show up and act.

Sangeeta Desarda grew up in a house where service was not an occasion — it was the daily weather. Her father, Professor Hiralal Desarda, was an economist who set aside position and title to devote his life to social welfare: championing farmers, fighting for water conservation, advocating tirelessly across India for the land and the people who live from it. Her mother, Shakuntala, a nutritionist, taught that sustainable food and farming could quietly transform a family's life. Their example cast a deep and lasting impression.

It followed her into the law — through more than a decade of practice at the Aurangabad Bench of the Bombay High Court, where she watched the people who most needed justice arrive least equipped to claim it.

And all the while, one truth kept sharpening: the world was not moving toward its Sustainable Development Goals — it was drifting away from them. A better world, she realised, does not happen on its own. Someone has to bring it about.

So during the COVID years, she committed herself to a single question: what can ordinary people do at the grassroots that ripples into something far bigger? Her answer, in August 2022, was Arihant Foundation — a name that, in an old Indian tradition, belongs to those who conquer their inner enemies. Fear. Apathy. Indifference.

At its heart lies her deepest conviction: education is the greatest leveler — the greatest edge this generation holds. Shared equally between girls and boys, it is how she believes every crisis of sustainability, from inequality to a warming planet, will finally be solved.

Today, Arihant Foundation is working to translate that belief into practical action — with communities, children, women and young people at the centre of the journey.

“Leadership is not about visibility alone, but impact.”
Adv. Sangeeta Hiralal Desarda
Founder & President
Advocate, Bombay High Court (Aurangabad Bench)
